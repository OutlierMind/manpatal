We spoke about nothing —
the kind of nothing
that later becomes everything.

==Some places remember people better than people do.==

![The lamp posts came on early that day.](images/that-evening-1.jpg)

It rained a little.
Neither of us mentioned the umbrella
sitting dry in my bag.

![Wet roads](images/that-evening-2.jpg)
![Two cups, one cold](images/that-evening-3.jpg){p}

Now the road is just a road.
But I walk it slower.
